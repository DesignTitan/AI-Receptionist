The logo

One mark, one word, one lockup. The flat mark is the 3D character reduced to what survives at any size: the rounded speech-bubble face, the tail, the smile. Files are in /public/brand; every SVG carries its own font, so the wordmark stays vector anywhere.

Colour variants

Pick by background, not by mood. Ink on light surfaces; white or mint on ink; mono black for print, engraving and single-colour print.

Favicon and app icon

The mark in mint on an ink tile, corners at 22%. It is the site favicon (src/app/icon.svg) and the home-screen icon (src/app/apple-icon.png). At 16px only the face and smile need to read, so nothing else is on the tile.

The character and the mark

Using it

  • Clear space: keep a margin of one mark-width on every side of the lockup, half a mark-width around the mark alone.
  • Minimum size: lockup 120px wide on screen, 25mm in print; mark 16px; wordmark 60px wide.
  • Backgrounds: ink on white, sage or photographs with a light area; white or mint on ink; on photographs, sit the lockup on a soft ink panel first.
  • Lockup spacing: mark and wordmark stay cap-aligned with one mark-width between them; scale them together, never separately.
  • Wordmark: always lowercase, always with ™, never re-set in another face or weight.
