# Secure account access — concept board

Static visual proposal generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Shows sign-in, email confirmation, MFA verification, enrollment, recovery and account security settings. Passkeys, recovery codes and session controls shown here require implementation and security review; the image does not represent deployed functionality. Signup remains through Plans.

## Prompt

Create a NEW visual design image showing the complete secure authentication experience for AI Receptionist, expanding the supplied sign-in mockup. Use the reference only as our visual identity and mascot reference. Produce a crisp high-resolution landscape design presentation board with SIX clearly separate screen mockups in a balanced THREE COLUMN by TWO ROW grid, each screen same size, ample gutters, warm light-gray outer canvas, small external labels 01 SIGN IN, 02 CHECK EMAIL, 03 VERIFY IDENTITY, 04 SECURE YOUR ACCOUNT, 05 ACCOUNT RECOVERY, 06 SECURITY SETTINGS. Tiny title at top outside screens: 'AI Receptionist · Account access concept'. Each screen is a realistic flat white website UI with small centered mascot/AI Receptionist header, dark forest #1e3a34 bold friendly Apfel Grotezk-like headings, Open Runde-like rounded body typography, sage #ebf0ec and mint #d5eee3 accents, thin #e2e9e1 borders, consistent 8px spacing, rounded 48px-high inputs and pill buttons, quiet reassuring whitespace. Prioritize legible short labels and coherent UX. This is one static visual concept board, NOT working screenshots. No browser chrome, no internal toolbar, no certifications or security guarantee claims.
SCREEN 01: 'Welcome back.' Small approved furry sage speech-bubble mascot beside heading, preserving pointed bottom-left nub, eyes and smile from reference. Prominent dark button with simple passkey symbol 'Continue with a passkey'. Divider 'or use email'. Labeled 'Email address' input 'you@yourbusiness.com'; outlined button 'Email me a sign-in link'. Microcopy 'Two-factor verification may follow.' Lower subtle 'New here? Explore plans' link. No passwords or direct free account signup.
SCREEN 02: small outlined envelope in mint circle; 'Check your inbox.' Body 'Open the sign-in link we sent to your email.' Smaller example 'bubs@willowstudio.com'. Secondary links 'Use a different email' and 'Resend link'. Quiet helper 'You may be asked to verify your identity next.' Do not imply that email bypasses two-factor verification.
SCREEN 03: 'One more check.' Copy 'Enter the code from your authenticator app.' SINGLE wide input labeled '6-digit code', placeholder '000 000', primary 'Verify and continue'. Secondary 'Use another method' and 'Can’t access your authenticator?' No instant email bypass.
SCREEN 04: 'Secure your account.' Body 'Choose how you’ll verify it’s you.' Two large stacked choice rows: 'Passkey' with recommended badge and helper 'Use your device or security key'; 'Authenticator app' with helper 'Use a rotating verification code'. First row selected. Primary 'Set up passkey'. Small informational panel 'Next: save your recovery codes somewhere safe.' Do not display QR codes or invented actual secrets. No skip button.
SCREEN 05: 'Let’s get you back in.' Body 'Use a saved recovery code to verify your identity.' Field 'Recovery code' placeholder 'Enter a recovery code'; primary 'Verify recovery code'. Secondary link 'Try another sign-in method'. Small bordered help block 'Lost all your methods?' and link 'Request account recovery'. Helper 'Recovery requires identity verification.' Do not suggest support can waive MFA or that email access alone restores account.
SCREEN 06: 'Sign-in & security'. Compact three real settings groups: 'Passkeys' showing 'MacBook · Added today' plus 'Add passkey'; 'Authenticator app' status 'Enabled' plus 'Manage'; 'Recovery codes' plus 'Generate new codes'. Section 'Your devices' with 'Chrome on Mac · This device' and outlined 'Sign out other devices'. Small helper 'Security changes require verification.' This last screen may include small circular B avatar but other five screens should not appear signed in. Keep consistency and sufficient readable text; no excessive decoration, no locks scattered everywhere. Retain our friendly familiar identity while showing all six security states at once.
