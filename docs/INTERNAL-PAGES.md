# Internal and staff page list

The marketing hamburger groups staff pages separately from public site links. Staff routes retain their existing authentication. Development pages appear only in the local preview and are loaded from the page index catalogue.

## Staff

- Appointments — `/admin`
- Customers — `/admin/customers`
- Feature suggestions — `/admin/roadmap`
- Account recovery requests — `/admin/account-recovery`
- Staff sign in — `/admin/login`

- Roadmap — `/__dev/pages?view=roadmap`
- User journey — `/__dev/journey`
- Images & videos — `/__dev/design/campaign-v4/`
- Mascot character sheets — `/__dev/design/multi-industry-v1/character-sheets.html`

Customer account, signup, booking and marketing pages are excluded from this internal-page group. Record-specific staff detail routes are reached from their respective staff lists.
