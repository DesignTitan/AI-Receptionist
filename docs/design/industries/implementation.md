# Industries gallery

The approved white gallery is implemented in `src/components/marketing/industry-gallery.tsx`, styled with the existing marketing CSS. It covers doctors, dental, healthcare, fitness, personal care, retail, pets, creative studios, home/auto services, professional services and lessons/coaching.

On desktop, vertical scrolling moves the photographs horizontally while the introduction and conversation area stay in view. One awake fuzzy mascot appears beside a white, rounded conversation card. The conversation changes with the leading industry; it does not play audio. Matching dark forest labels, mint dots, fine sage borders and Open Runde / Apfel Grotezk typography follow the existing live-demo styling.

Phones and reduced-motion settings use a native horizontally scrollable gallery with scroll snapping. Previous/next buttons and arrow keys reach every industry. Reduced motion also removes the conversation entrance animation. Booking conversations are illustrative connected-pilot examples; confirmation examples retain setup/minutes context. Retail demonstrates showroom appointments rather than unsupported inventory lookups.

The film at `#turn` is the separately completed Happy Paws integration, with native video controls and English captions. A possible booking → call → outcome explainer immediately after it remains a recommendation, not an implemented new section.

Artwork is generated illustrative photography, optimized as WebP for the site. The character is an image asset based on the approved v3 face, with its entrance animated in CSS; it is not a live 3D model or a speaking audio agent.
