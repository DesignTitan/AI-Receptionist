She has plans, too

Back to the ringing phone

Return to present · Eyeline match

ACTIONShe glances from the phone to her soapy hands. She keeps the dog secure and returns to the wash. No exaggerated panic.
DIALOGUE / AUDIONARRATOR: “Someone needs you here. Someone needs you on the phone.”
MOTION / ON-SCREENSecond ring. Small “Incoming call” glass pill.
Make the competing demands visible; preserve her competence.
0414–19

The other side of the call

Reverse scene · Customer at home

ACTIONA customer waits with her terrier, Milo. She checks the time. She wants to fit a groom into her week.
DIALOGUE / AUDIORoom tone and a short ringback; no voice-over.
MOTION / ON-SCREENNo warning counters or fabricated lost-revenue amounts.
Give the caller a human need, not just a sales-lead label.
0519–25

A reassuring answer

Return to groomer · Product reveal

ACTIONThe open-eyed mascot appears beside the work scene. Ringing resolves into a calm voice; the owner continues washing.
DIALOGUE / AUDIOAI: “Hi, I’m the AI receptionist for the grooming studio. How can I help?”
MOTION / ON-SCREENSmall liquid-glass surface; eyes attentive, subtle blink and speech-synced mouth.
Make the product directly resolve the opening interruption.
0625–32

A specific request

Customer reaction · Natural dialogue

ACTIONThe caller strokes Milo and explains what she needs. We see why she called rather than watch an abstract interface.
DIALOGUE / AUDIOCUSTOMER: “Could I book Milo for his usual groom on Thursday? After lunch would be ideal.”
MOTION / ON-SCREENOne short caption at a time. Returning-customer example; details established off-screen.
Show a meaningful request and preference, not a robotic yes/no exchange.

0732–39

An answer that fits

Product insert · Available time

ACTIONThe AI presents a suitable available afternoon slot. Keep the owner visible in the background; this happens alongside her work.
DIALOGUE / AUDIOAI: “There’s a two o’clock appointment available on Thursday. Would you like that one?”
MOTION / ON-SCREENHighlight “Thursday · 2 PM”; do not make the caller navigate the interface.
Demonstrate contextual usefulness with one understandable decision.
0839–44

The customer chooses

Customer close-up · Relief

ACTIONShe nods. Her shoulders relax. She can move on with her day, too.
DIALOGUE / AUDIOCUSTOMER: “Two is perfect. Thank you.”
MOTION / ON-SCREENThe selected time settles gently into place. No narrator over dialogue.
Give the customer agency and an observable outcome.
0944–51

Both customers taken care of

Match cut · Towel drying

ACTIONThe owner dries the dog. Beside her, the new appointment resolves into a confirmation. The two threads meet.
DIALOGUE / AUDIOAI: “You’re all set. Milo is booked for Thursday at two. See you then.”
MOTION / ON-SCREENBooking card, drawn check, three brief celebration strokes. Quiet confirmation tone.
Pay off the product promise in the same real-world scene.
1051–57

It could be your business

One short parallel example · Photographer

ACTIONA photographer keeps guiding a portrait customer as the same discreet answering cue appears. One new setting, no feature montage.
DIALOGUE / AUDIONARRATOR: “When your work needs your attention, your next customer still gets an answer.”
MOTION / ON-SCREENSame mascot and glass treatment. No claim of unlimited simultaneous calls.
Broaden relevance beyond grooming without abandoning the central idea.
1157–64

She keeps her plan

Return to owner · Visual callback

ACTIONShe collects the hiking bag we saw earlier and heads out. Show an ordinary finish to the day, not a miraculous business transformation.
DIALOGUE / AUDIOOWNER, voice message: “On my way.” The studio door clicks shut.
MOTION / ON-SCREENMusic opens up gently; no new notifications chasing her out.
Resolve the personal goal with an action rather than another slogan.

A life beyond the next ring

Wide lakeside shot · Hold the ending

ACTIONThe original lakeside couple and dog. She is present with them, phone put away. Let this shot breathe, then add the invitation.

End with earned relief and one clear next step.
