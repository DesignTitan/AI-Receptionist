# A life beyond the next ring

72-second film — final scene artwork pass. Happy Paws Grooming is a fictional demo business.

## 01. Meet the customer in her hands — 00–04

Open on the beautiful Samoyed’s whole face: upright ears, a goofy natural grin. The groomer is already washing him with both hands.

Water and gentle room tone. The phone begins to ring.

## 02. Both hands. One very good dog. — 04–09

The Samoyed tilts his head as her soapy hands work through his coat. Stay in the present. She has not stopped grooming.

NARRATOR: “It always rings when your hands are full.”

## 03. She hears it. She can’t pick up. — 09–14

She glances at the ringing phone on the dry counter. Both hands stay wet and occupied with the dog; she returns her attention to him.

NARRATOR: “And there’s someone on the other end who needs you, too.”

## 04. Milo could use a little help — 14–19

Milo sits beside his customer, with the wild-haired reference guiding his appearance. She calls the groomer.

CUSTOMER, affectionately to Milo: “We’re getting you a haircut.”
A short ringback.

## 05. A reassuring answer — 19–25

The open-eyed mascot answers beside the studio scene. The groomer stays quiet and keeps washing; the AI is speaking to the caller.

AI: “Hi, Happy Paws Grooming. How can I help?”

## 06. A specific request — 25–32

Cut to the same caller, speaking naturally. Milo stays off-screen rather than changing appearance.

CUSTOMER: “Could I book Milo for his usual groom on Thursday? After lunch would be ideal.”

## 07. An answer that fits — 32–39

The AI presents a suitable available afternoon slot. Keep the owner visible in the background; this happens alongside her work.

AI: “There’s a two o’clock appointment available on Thursday. Would you like that one?”

## 08. The customer chooses — 39–44

The same caller smiles as she accepts the offered appointment.

CUSTOMER: “Two is perfect. Thank you.”

## 09. Both customers taken care of — 44–51

The owner dries the dog. Beside her, the new appointment resolves into a confirmation. The two threads meet.

AI: “You’re all set. Milo is booked for Thursday at two. See you then.”

## 10. The work is finished — 51–57

The appointment has finished. She puts away her apron, ready to leave. This is the first time her hands are free.

NARRATOR: “The appointment’s booked. One less call to return before you head out.”

## 11. Time for her plans — 57–64

Work finished, the groomer heads outside with her hiking bag.

NARRATOR: “Because you have plans, too.”
OWNER, voice message: “On my way.”

## 12. A life beyond the next ring — 64–72

NARRATOR: “Be there for your customers. And for yourself. Meet your AI receptionist.”
