04 / A clear way to get started

Start with the way your business works.

Your phone number. Your team. Your appointment book. We review the details with you before incoming AI answering goes live.

  1. Tell us about your business.

    Add your team, phone provider and the place you manage appointments. “Not sure” is a starting point, too.

  2. Choose the help you want.

    Select a plan and prepare your answering choices, confirmation calls and working hours.

  3. Test the setup together.

    We check the phone connection and appointment book, then test the call and booking flow before activation.

Can customers keep using our current number?

That’s the aim. We check your phone provider and service first, then agree the connection steps with you. Choosing a provider during signup saves your details; it doesn’t change your phone routing.

Will it work with our existing booking software?

The current booking page uses AI Receptionist’s own appointment book. Existing booking software needs a compatibility review and does not automatically sync. Tell us what you use before switching.

Can I switch AI answering off?

Yes. On a connected line, switching incoming routing off sends new calls to your configured staff destination or voicemail. It doesn’t undo forwarding set with your phone provider. Outgoing confirmation calls have their own switch.
