# Human benefit photograph — Higgsfield revision

Bubs rejected the earlier technical imagery and the artificial finish of the coastal terrace image. The useful direction is an owner getting time back, shown through a credible human moment. This revision focuses on one candid coastal-café photograph before further scene or motion work.

## Model selection

Bubs explicitly requested Higgsfield and “Canto 2.5.” The live unfiltered catalogue returned no exact Canto model. Cinema Studio Image 2.5 is the matching still-image entry, supports square images and offers 4K output. A clarification was requested; generation proceeded with the stated interpretation of Cinema Studio Image 2.5. This is not a verified alias for Canto.

The requested model is `cinematic_studio_2_5`, at `4k`, aspect ratio `1:1`, one image. The exact request is saved in `docs/generation-request.json`. Generation uses the Higgsfield connector, not the built-in image generator. No provider can guarantee perfect photographic realism; the actual result must be inspected.

## Photographic direction

- An ordinary business owner taking a quiet coffee break at a real coastal café.
- Readable candid profile, relaxed posture, cup resting on the table, casually placed phone.
- Soft natural side light, coherent perspective, natural skin and fabric, restrained processing.
- A refined real environment with modest signs of use; no showroom styling, AI interfaces or rendered devices.

The product message remains: customers book online, AI calls them to confirm, and the owner sees the outcome. The visual is a proposed illustration of time back, not a customer testimonial. Preserve prior files as history; do not treat the rejected imagery as approved marketing material.

## Result and finishing

Cinema Studio Image 2.5 returned a 4096 × 4096 PNG. Its face, hands and skin/fabric detail were visually inspected. The first generation added an unrequested decorative film border. A targeted generative border edit failed, with no useful reason returned by Higgsfield; its request and failed result are retained in `docs/`.

The final version removes only 40 pixels from each outer edge using ImageMagick in Higgsfield's own cloud sandbox. This preserves the photograph's remaining pixels without regeneration or resampling. The final image is 4016 × 4016; the untouched 4096 × 4096 source is retained.
