# Business story series

Finish and approve the grooming story first. Bubs confirmed that photography, salons, mechanics and other appointment businesses should each receive their own complete video, rather than being inserted as unrelated cutaways in this film.

Shared elements: recognizable work interruption, a caller with a specific need, AI assistance with an understandable outcome, then a personal payoff. Keep the fuzzy open-eyed mascot and restrained liquid-glass styling consistent. Each industry needs its own characters, task, realistic booking requirements and ending.

- Photographer: cannot interrupt directing a portrait session.
- Salon: hands occupied working with the current customer.
- Mechanic: hands occupied during a repair; scheduling is distinct from diagnosis, price guarantees or promising repair completion.
- Additional businesses: select later; no generation batch authorized by this planning note alone.

Do not generate the series until the first story’s direction is settled. Validate each scenario against the product’s actual capabilities and setup requirements.
