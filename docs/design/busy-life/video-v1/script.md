# Happy Paws — first moving edit

Target: 70 seconds, 16:9, 1080p. Approved cast and mischievous Milo. Generated advertising dialogue, not a recording of the live product.

1. **0–10s — Hands full.** Open on the Samoyed, wash and close-up. Narrator: “It always rings when your hands are full.” Phone begins ringing near the end. Both hands remain occupied.
2. **10–20s — The AI answers.** Groomer glances toward the phone. Ring stops; fuzzy mascot springs into view beside a small glass speech bubble. Its mouth moves as it says: “Hi, Happy Paws Grooming. How can I help?” It settles into a listening pose.
3. **20–30s — Milo’s customer.** Crazy-haired Milo tugs at the blanket. Caller: “Could I book Milo for his usual groom on Thursday? After lunch would be ideal.”
4. **30–39s — A useful answer.** Cut back to the working groomer and talking mascot. AI: “There’s a two o’clock appointment available on Thursday. Would you like that one?” A Thursday / 2 PM choice appears beside the mascot.
5. **39–44s — Customer chooses.** Caller: “Two is perfect. Thank you.”
6. **44–53s — Booked.** Groomer towel dries the Samoyed. Talking mascot: “You’re all set. Milo is booked for Thursday at two. See you then.” Small booking check appears; mascot settles.
7. **53–70s — Time back.** Fold apron, leave work, meet partner by lake. Narrator: “The appointment’s booked. One less call to return. And time for the plans you made.” Pause. “Be there for your customers. And for yourself. Meet your AI receptionist.”

Mascot direction: open glossy eyes, fuzzy mint speech-bubble body, subtle blinking and bobbing, mouth movement during AI speech only. Outside the glass bubble, as in Bubs’s supplied screenshots. No large dashboard panel. Voiceover never competes with the conversation. Dialogue continuity and generated mouth movement require inspection before release.

Incoming phone booking is a connected-pilot scenario. Happy Paws is a fictional business. Returning-customer details are assumed established; this edit is shortened for the advertisement.
