## Research and direction

- Samoyed: natural upward mouth corners, erect ears, white ruff and a slight head tilt. Wet body fur must clump realistically while the face stays fluffy. Reference: https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/lifestyle/fun-facts-samoyed/ and https://www.dogbreedinfo.com/samoyed.htm
- Scene 4 first choice: Polish Lowland Sheepdog with an uneven fringe and scruffy beard; the haircut joke has a visible reason. Photo examples (including tilted head): https://www.dogbreedinfo.com/polishlowlandsheepdog.htm
- Scene 4 alternative: Old English Sheepdog, larger shaggy silhouette and peekaboo hair. https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/old-english-sheepdog/
- Web text and photo descriptions reviewed; direct image retrieval failed. These pages are inspiration sources, not licensed production assets.
